# DynaMol 1.0.0 — first stable source release

DynaMol 1.0.0 is the first release whose source, documentation and evidence
are published together as a stable baseline: the local molecular dynamics
workbench with bundled-engine support (OpenMM and GROMACS), protein and
protein–ligand preparation, checkpoint recovery, persistent workspaces,
trajectory exploration and measurement analysis described in the
[user guide](../../README.md).

## What changed since 0.1.1

- **Loop repair hardening.** Observed peptide neighbours of a rebuilt loop
  keep their preliminary sidechain coordinates until the dedicated loop
  refinement, so the refinement's 1 Å movement limit is measured against the
  real reference. Newly built sidechain branches are corrected to standard
  stereochemistry with all observed atoms held fixed, followed by a
  complete-environment collision check. Loop refinement records its input,
  final and topology artifacts with hashes, distinguishes provisional loop
  joins from breaks between observed residues, and reports a numerical
  blow-up during minimization as an explicit loop rejection instead of raw
  engine text. See [loop repair](../LOOP_REPAIR.md).
- **Staged loop construction.** 0.1.1 searched the ProMod3 fragment database
  with the original stems only. 1.0.0 retries sparse fragment matches, then
  one and two neighbouring residues of temporary construction context, then a
  bounded native Monte Carlo attempt, always exporting only the originally
  missing atoms. The preparation monitor shows the active stage, and the
  recorded provenance keeps every attempt, including discarded context
  displacements. A difficult closure may also receive one recorded,
  restrained relaxation of the immediate protein flanks within 1 Å.
- **Standalone Mac window candidate.** The checkout builds a native window
  with a Dock icon and file dialogs around the same bundled engines
  ([details](../NATIVE_MAC.md)); the published 0.1.1 installer still opens
  the interface in the default browser.
- **Preparation and identity fixes** carried over from the September panel
  work: source label/author chain mapping, exact ligand connection identities,
  insertion-code preservation, a declared sulfate reference state, and
  ligand preparation with zero newly added hydrogens.
- **Research code is published, not enabled.** The bounded candidate-search
  loop workflow with a CCTBX backbone-reference gate, the advanced covalent
  and metal-site parameter work, and the QM worker live under
  `docs/audit/` and `backend/` as inspectable research paths. None of them
  changes the application's behaviour; their evidence directories document
  what was and was not achieved.

## Evidence for this release

- Backend suite on the released source: **1,042 passed, 0 failed, 15
  skipped** (12 opt-in PySCF numerical checks, 3 optional audit fixtures).
- Frontend TypeScript and Vite production build.
- Frozen loop panel through the real preparation worker: **7 of 9 metal-free
  repair candidates prepared**, each passing 20 software, identity, geometry
  and static force-field checks, including both 12-residue examples (1UA2,
  1M17) and the four-gap 8K5R. 2CG9 and 3HEG were rejected by the acceptance
  gates; covalent-glycan, incomplete-ligand and 13-residue cases remain
  explicit refusals; the ATP–Mg case is deferred. See the
  [recheck](../audit/loop-fallback/REPORT.md#v100-recheck-on-the-released-source).
- Isolated browser regressions: 119 Playwright cases against a disposable
  data root and the real API, engines and WebGL viewer. **105 passed**, 7
  skipped (they need documented native-run fixtures), 7 failed. One failing
  spec carried two expectations stale since the 11 September UI changes and
  passes after correction. Of the remaining six, three predate 11 September
  behaviour changes (completed followed runs now open automatically in
  Explore, so the old dynamics-card assertions look for a dialog that has
  closed; a generic `LIG` residue is now checked against the CCD), one needs
  an undocumented 6A93 fixture, and two are WebGL pixel-count and animation
  thresholds missed by 0.5% and 2.4%. The job behind the GROMACS case
  completed in 14 s. None indicates a behaviour regression; the stale specs
  are listed below for follow-up.

## Known limitations

- **Rebuilt loops are rough starting coordinates.** The acceptance gates test
  geometry, chirality and collisions, not backbone conformation. An
  independent CCTBX Ramachandran check found outliers among modeled residues
  in **4 of the 7** accepted panel loops (1UA2: 3 of 12 residues, 8K5R: 5 of
  17, 1M17: 1 of 12, 2HYY: 1 of 4). Inspect rebuilt regions, minimize and
  equilibrate before analysis, and supply a complete model when the region
  matters. A stricter workflow that passes 6 of 9 cases on all eight complete
  checks exists as research code and is the planned loop change after 1.0.0;
  it depends on a CCTBX runtime the app does not bundle.
- **Mac installer.** The 0.1.1 DMG remains the current application download.
  A 1.0.0 installer needs its own packaging run and acceptance record before
  it is attached; the source release does not change an installed 0.1.1 app.
  The installer has an ad-hoc integrity signature without Developer ID
  signing or notarization; follow
  [Apple's per-app opening guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).
- **Scope.** CPU execution with a 100,000-atom packaged limit; membranes,
  GROMACS complex-parameter transfer, covalent ligand attachments and
  metal-site models are outside this release and produce explicit blockers.
  Software and gross geometry checks are not evidence of physical
  convergence, force-field accuracy or biological stability.

## Source layout note

Bulk audit evidence (per-run workspaces, trajectories, topologies and
serialized force-field systems, about 1.4 GB) is kept out of the repository;
the tracked reports, scripts and compact summaries record the SHA-256 of
every offloaded artifact they cite.
