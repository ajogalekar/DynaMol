# Published boronate sources: applicability to the frozen human 5LF3 case

The source search identified useful references, but not a complete parameter set
that can currently be admitted for the N-terminal Thr–bortezomib adduct. The
frozen case remains unresolved; no molecular graph, charge state, force field,
panel member or original component was changed, and no calculation launched.

Chiacchio et al. report Amber parameters for four model boronates bound to a
serine side chain, including fitted torsions and literature bond/angle terms.
Their validation concerns those models. It does not directly establish the
joint charge distribution or attachment parameters of bortezomib bound to an
N-terminal threonine. [Primary article](https://www.mdpi.com/1420-3049/28/6/2866).

The retrieved Europe PMC article XML and complete supplementary archive are
retained with hashes. The nested supplementary archive contains one four-page
PDF of torsion-history plots, with no separate Amber topology, residue-template,
charge, or parameter files. This is an inventory of the retrieved supplement,
not a claim that no relevant parameters appear in the article's tables or exist
elsewhere. Those tables are a lead for explicit applicability review, not a
ready-to-load full-adduct model. The article declares CC BY 4.0.

Chen et al. investigate bortezomib/proteasome reaction states. Their classical MD
describes an initial complex before covalent attachment; subsequent QM/MM treats
bond formation and proton transfer. Consequently, its free-ligand boron
parameterization does not by itself validate a classical covalent-product model.
The proposed product protonation also needs to be distinguished from other
boronate/Thr states. The linked supporting PDF could not be retrieved (HTTP403),
so no completeness claim is made about it.
[Primary study](https://global-sci.com/index.php/cicc/article/view/23424).

Next work is to compare the explicit product graph, proton locations, charge
and terminal context against the frozen 5LF3 source, then determine whether any
published terms apply and identify the remaining joint-charge/interface work.
No neutral trigonal-boron model should be admitted as a tetrahedral product.
