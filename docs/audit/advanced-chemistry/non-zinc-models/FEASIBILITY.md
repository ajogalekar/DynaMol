# Published models for the four difficult metal families

**Verdict:** one concrete His-ligated oxyheme package is available for a controlled 1A6M prototype. It is not yet a validated DynaMol model. No complete, reusable parameter package matching the exact frozen state of the other three cases was verified in this bounded search. None of these four cases has newly passed preparation or dynamics. No QM or MD was launched.

The frozen inputs and their author/label atom identities remain in [target-sites.json](target-sites.json). Exact retrieved bytes, URLs, dates, checksums, and declared licenses are recorded in `retrieval-*.json`; `artifact-manifest.json` hashes the complete evidence directory. This is a source/applicability audit, not a force-field accuracy certification.

**Rejected shortcuts.** The public [CuCuSOD1 parameter repository](https://github.com/Mag14011/CuCuSOD1_SI_CompMethods) was pinned at `fae9b1add7843d19e331786395475092e119ff95`, and representative `.lib`/`.frcmod` files were downloaded. It describes **Cu replacing Zn**, with separate deliberately broken donor contacts for constant-pH modeling. Its `ZnSite` directory name does not make the metal Zn. No license file was present and GitHub reported no license. These files are retained as an explicit mismatch, not accepted parameters. No independent redistribution license was verified for the Manchester or CHARMM-GUI source collections; their public accessibility is not treated as a license. The old [Manchester Amber heme collection](https://personalpages.manchester.ac.uk/staff/Richard.Bryce/amber/cof/frcmod.hemall) was also retrieved: it has ligand-bound Hb/Mb bonded terms, but the accompanying `heme_all.in` alone has no complete O2/proximal-His coupled library. It is not a verified ff14SB oxy-Mb solution; current retrieved file does include NP/NO Lennard–Jones entries. Cys-ligated P450, metal substitution, deletion of observed components, and added geometric restraints were not used as substitutes.

**Exact oxy-myoglobin findings.** The deposited `par_all27.inp` header actually identifies CHARMM22 proteins plus CHARMM27 lipids. Its `e14fac 1.0` means unscaled 1–4 electrostatics; atom-specific CHARMM 1–4 Lennard–Jones parameters and exclusions must remain. Its TIP3P has Lennard–Jones terms on water hydrogens. This entire Hamiltonian must remain separate from Amber ff14SB/GAFF, Amber water conventions, and Amber 1–4 scaling.

`top_heme_oxy_optimized_charges.inp` assigns HEM −1.50 and OXY −0.50, with Fe partial charge +1.42 and oxygen charges −0.18/−0.32. The baseline assigns HEM −2 and neutral OXY with ±0.021. **The supplied construction script loads the baseline, and the deposited 10-ns PSF contains the baseline charges.** The updated file may be a later addition, but that chronology was not established. Thus the deposited trajectory cannot be claimed to validate the updated-charge model. The original files are immutable; [the diff](dryad-oxy-myoglobin/topology-charge-diff.txt) and [static inspection](source-inspection.json) expose the discrepancy.

Both versions use `PHEM` for His NE2–Fe and `PLO2` for Fe–O1. They contain an explicit **zero** O2–O1–Fe angle term and **zero** O2–O1–Fe–porphyrin torsion. These are genuine source values, not missing parameters. Other terms remain: Fe–O1 and O–O stretching, Fe–porphyrin and Fe–His geometry, oxygen/porphyrin angles, and environment-dependent nonbonded interactions with the published exclusions. A zero local angle term does not mean the entire angular potential is zero. Nor do intact bonds establish accuracy. No parameter was changed. The original 1A6M Fe–O1–O2 angle is approximately 122.53°, O–O 1.235 Å; those geometric observations do not define a replacement force constant.

**Concrete next step for 1A6M, without starting calculations now:**

1. Preserve assembly1 and all its observed components: protein A, HEM A154, OXY A157, **both SO4 A155/A156**, and crystallographic waters. Record alternate-location selection and every added hydrogen. Keep source identity maps independent of CHARMM residue/segment aliases.
2. Build a standalone CHARMM PSF/parameter provenance bundle, naming the baseline and updated-charge model as distinct versions. Select the updated topology explicitly in a derived script; never overwrite the deposited script. Map His93 to neutral Nδ-protonated HSD, whose free NE2 binds Fe. Preserve heme propionate state and the source end-on oxygen atom mapping. Review the additional deposited Fe–O2 connection as coordination evidence, not a second automatically generated covalent bond.
3. Obtain and validate an all-atom sulfate model compatible with this CHARMM Hamiltonian. The deposited topology lacks SO4. A legacy united-atom CHARMM19 SO4 entry is not a sufficient substitute. Do not delete or neutralize the sulfates to obtain a passing build. Resolve complete protonation/charge and water-model accounting before building a runnable system.
4. Reproduce exact native bonded terms, exclusions, 1–4 interactions, charges, Lennard–Jones values and masses against the imported OpenMM/GROMACS representation. Preserve the six native duplicate-improper warnings and establish the intended precedence. The static inspection currently parses the files but creates no System or simulation Context.
5. Only after a complete build, evaluate the **whole-site** angular and torsional energy behavior against independent reference evidence, along with metal/oxygen coordination, heme planarity and proximal/distal histidine interactions. Keep the published zero terms unless a separately justified and validated new model is declared. A short stable trajectory would be a subsequent workflow check, not evidence of redox chemistry or physical accuracy.

The other families remain parameter-development or exact-source-retrieval tasks. These negative findings describe this bounded search; they do not establish that no usable model exists anywhere.
