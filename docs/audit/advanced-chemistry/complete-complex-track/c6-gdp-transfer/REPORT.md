# c6 and GDP transfer evidence — 14 September 2026

**The inspected official sources still do not supply a `c6` polarizability mapping. GDP's guanine base is closely matched to the Amber guanosine reference, but transfer of the fitted ion corrections to the complete GDP(−3)/protein site remains unvalidated.** This audit narrows the two issues; it does not assign parameters or qualify the modified 12-6-4 model for DynaMol.

The [machine-readable evidence](evidence.json) is generated by [the read-only source inspection](inspect_transfer.py). All 26 recorded inputs were rehashed after the inspection and remained unchanged. The original diagnostics, topology, and fixed covalent/metal panels are preserved.

## c6: explicit lookup still absent

The installed GAFF2 2.2.20 definition identifies `c6` as an sp³ carbon in a six-membered ring. Current official GAFF2 2.2.30 retains that definition. Read-only native connectivity inspection confirms all four affected atoms have four bonded neighbors and belong to six-membered heavy-atom cycles:

| Native index, zero-based | Native name | Deposited drug atom | Native charge, e |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 191 | C013 | MOV C19 | 0.087275 |
| 192 | C012 | MOV C20 | 0.224239 |
| 241 | C020 | MOV C17 | 0.195441 |
| 242 | C019 | MOV C18 | 0.073227 |

This establishes chemical context independently of the type's name; no bond order or replacement parameter was inferred. [Pinned official GAFF2 source](https://github.com/Amber-MD/AmberClassic/blob/8e55e97ada48b96eefaec2e6a3fa849018aaeea5/dat/leap/parm/gaff2.dat).

The current official `lj_1264_pol.dat` is byte-identical to the installed table, SHA256 `9a64b54fc4ba6b683bb9ea1bd51b5f6d36793cce53d3a5e54838c719722c7429`. Both omit `c6`. The inspected `add1264.py` performs an exact atom-type lookup and raises an error if any member of a shared nonbonded class lacks a value. It has no fallback based on element, hybridization, a similar type name, or equal Lennard–Jones parameters. [Pinned polarizability table](https://github.com/Amber-MD/AmberClassic/blob/8e55e97ada48b96eefaec2e6a3fa849018aaeea5/dat/leap/parm/lj_1264_pol.dat).

Two shortcuts would be unjustified:

- The GAFF2 mass table's third-column value for `c6` is 0.360. The assessed C4 workflow reads its separate polarizability table; it does not use this value. For `c3`, the corresponding two numbers are 0.878 and 1.061, demonstrating that the tables are not interchangeable.
- A generic sp³-carbon rule would not reproduce all existing C4 entries. GAFF2 describes `cx` and `cy` as small-ring sp³ carbons, yet their listed C4-source values are 1.352, whereas `c3` is 1.061. No universal hybridization alias was documented in the inspected implementation.

Miller's paper is the attribution given by the table. Its publisher record was verified, but its full text was unavailable during this bounded review. No detailed Miller-to-GAFF2 mapping is claimed from the citation alone. [Miller, 1990](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja00179a044).

**Decision:** explicit source mapping not found in the installed package, current official table, implementation, tutorial, or targeted official-source searches. `c6` remains unassigned. This is a lookup/provenance gap, not evidence that a defensible carbon mapping cannot be developed.

## GDP: local base agreement, different phosphate model

The unchanged native complex retains one 40-atom GDP with net charge −3 e and its recorded Meagher–Redman–Carlson model. Its scoped type aliases were reversed using the saved mapping only.

All **15 guanine-base atomic charges** agree with Amber's neutral guanosine `GN` template within 5×10⁻⁹ e, including N7 = −0.5709 e and O6 = −0.5597 e. Fourteen of fifteen atom-type names also agree after resolving the recorded aliases. C8 differs (`CK` versus the reference's `CP`); both have the same listed default polarizability, but no broader parameter equivalence is inferred. The two legacy hydrogen-name matches were checked against their unique bonded heavy atom.

The ff14SB loader explicitly loads `nucleic12.lib`, which contains `GN`. Current official and installed copies of that library match byte-for-byte. This is a reproducible **Amber reference comparison**, not recovery of the exact topology used by the 2015 authors. [Pinned ff14SB loader](https://github.com/Amber-MD/AmberClassic/blob/8e55e97ada48b96eefaec2e6a3fa849018aaeea5/dat/leap/cmd/oldff/leaprc.ff14SB), [pinned nucleic-acid templates](https://github.com/Amber-MD/AmberClassic/blob/8e55e97ada48b96eefaec2e6a3fa849018aaeea5/dat/leap/lib/nucleic12.lib).

This agreement provides positive local evidence for assessing a guanosine-N7 correction. It does **not** establish the same binding free energy for neutral guanosine and GDP(−3), which adds a charged diphosphate and sits in a complete protein/metal/water environment.

The retained nonbridging phosphate atoms are:

| Native GDP atoms | Original type | Charge per atom, e | Documented default α, Å³ | Fitted DMP transfer |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| O1A, O2A | O2 | −0.9474 | 0.569 | Unvalidated |
| O1B, O2B, O3B | O3 | −0.9552 | 0.569 | Unvalidated |

Panteva and colleagues fit selected dimethyl-phosphate and nucleoside interactions in TIP4P-Ew; their DMP parameters came from Dupradeau and colleagues. The paper and SI inspected here do not establish a fitted correction for the retained Meagher GDP(−3) model. The exact DMP charge artifact was not recovered, so no numerical DMP-versus-GDP donor-charge difference is invented. [Panteva primary paper](https://theory.rutgers.edu/resources/pdfs/Panteva_JChemPhysB_2015_v119_p15460.pdf), [Dupradeau source reference](https://doi.org/10.1039/C0CP00111B), [Meagher GDP/polyphosphate source](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcc.10262).

The official Amber tutorial makes the implementation boundary concrete. Its modified-model example uses DNA 1D23, selects purine N7 atoms, and selects phosphate atoms named `OP*`. That phosphate pattern matches **none** of the actual GDP atoms listed above. Its guanine residue-name pattern would match GDP's N7, but a naming match is not evidence of chemical transfer. The tutorial also splits the selected nonbonded types before installing the correction. [Official tutorial, Example B](https://ambermd.org/tutorials/advanced/tutorial20/12_6_4.php).

**Decision:** the documented default rule covers GDP O2/O3/NB after the existing alias resolution. Missing GDP-specific fitted validation does not erase that mathematical coverage. Substituting the DMP or guanosine fitted corrections remains a separate, unqualified transfer decision.

## Provenance, limits, and next step

New official files are pinned at AmberClassic commit `8e55e97ada48b96eefaec2e6a3fa849018aaeea5`; URLs, hashes, versions, and comparison rows are in the evidence JSON. Research downloads are retained at:

The apparent Meagher PDF download returned HTML and is explicitly excluded as full-text evidence. The R.E.D. supporting-information endpoint returned HTTP 429; PMC returned a browser challenge. Neither was bypassed. The previously available Panteva paper and SI were used from their verified local copies. Publication/SI rights remain separate from the installed Amber `dat/leap` public-domain grant; the new AmberClassic root license concerns its code and was not treated as a new blanket data license.

The complete system remains 27,477 atoms: protein, covalent sotorasib–cysteine unit, GDP, Mg, retained/bulk waters, and sodium counterions. No component, charge, parameter, topology, or app behavior changed. The existing water-family and exact GROMACS C4-representation gaps remain open.

**Next bounded step:** recover an explicit carbon-class crosswalk for the missing `c6` entry and the exact DMP/guanosine fit topology/charge artifacts. A subsequent comparison can then distinguish a documented baseline extension from a new fitted-correction transfer before building any modified full-complex static-test topology. Do not treat base-charge agreement, a tutorial wildcard, or a successful parameter-file parse as complete-complex validation.
