# Five-system pressure test: selection and preflight review

The selected benchmark is **1XQZ, 1SUG, 3PTB, 4BVN and 1K4C**: two GROMACS and three OpenMM cases, each targeting 100 ps of production after 10 ps of relaxation. The user explicitly approved water-only protein boxes for this version. The GPCR/channel cases therefore test software handling of membrane-protein constructs without a membrane; they cannot support biological interpretations of receptor activity, channel gating, permeation or membrane stability.

The initial PatAgent preflight returned **proceed: four applicable gates passed, zero failed or unresolved**. The manifest additionally records per-case structural and chemistry gates that the general checklist does not encode. Both soluble GROMACS cases passed actual application dry validation and have hashed selected-input reports. The OpenMM cases require their recorded preparation, assembly and occupancy checks before production. No dynamics was launched by this reviewer.

## Channel assembly and occupancy

The 1K4C source identifies channel polymer entity 3, author chain C. Assembly 1 applies operators 1–4. Apply those operators to the channel polymer to create four subunits. The seven deposited K records sit close to the fourfold axis and each has occupancy 0.25; transforming every ion four times would create nearly coincident duplicates. The occupancy values are consistent with special-position symmetry and should not be interpreted as direct biological site occupancy.

A practical **declared software starting configuration** retains original K residues 3002, 3004 and 3005 exactly once, at source coordinates (155.331,155.331,−33.953), (155.330,155.324,−40.505) and (155.327,155.335,−47.577) Å. The closest K–K separation is 6.552 Å. Explicitly record exclusion of 3001/3003/3006/3007 as a chosen discrete configuration, not an experimentally demonstrated simultaneous occupancy. Do not infer missing experimental waters. Source coordinates, occupancies and assembly operators are in `review-channel-ions.json`.

## Findings and claim limits

The real 3PTB dry inspection initially rejected BEN because its neutral CCD reference contains a terminal C=NH E/Z descriptor that cannot be verified from its heavy-atom geometry by RDKit. [RDKit documents the heavy-neighbor criterion for its stereogenic-bond perception](https://www.rdkit.org/docs/RDKit_Book.html#stereogenic-bonds). The reviewed change only treats neutral, acyclic C=NH with degree-one nitrogen, one hydrogen and STEREONONE as **unverified**, retaining a warning and the unchanged pose. Opposite or unresolved observable alkene/substituted-imine stereo and contradictory explicit overrides remain errors. Eight initial focused cases passed independently; the implementation owner subsequently added reverse-direction controls and reported 12 passes. This supports correcting a representation error, not claiming new stereochemical evidence. Native preparation must record BEN's selected pH state and parameters.

Current DynaMol has no membrane builder, lipid preset, prepared-state transfer to GROMACS or validated external membrane-topology execution path. That missing capability is a product gap. The existing OpenMM and GROMACS force-field presets also differ, so these runs are not a controlled ranking of engines. A finite trajectory or modest RMSD over 100 ps cannot establish convergence, meaningful uncertainty or biological stability.

Rejected alternatives are retained as selection evidence: CDK2 1HCL has four unresolved internal residues and would require preparation before GROMACS; 2RH1 contains recorded CYS–ACM and CYS–PLM covalent links; 4EIY contains a 15-residue unresolved internal fusion-protein segment above the existing loop limit. Replacing candidates is recorded rather than silently deleting these issues.
