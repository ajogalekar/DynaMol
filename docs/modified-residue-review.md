Ten [missing-sequence regressions](../backend/tests/test_missing_modified_sequence.py) verify that absent SEP/TPO/PTR/HYP and unknown amino-acid identities remain unchanged, including when ligand removal is selected. PDBFixer's implicit parent substitution is bypassed locally without altering its global behavior. Internal gaps containing these residues are blocked from construction; ordinary supported short-loop building remains available. Submission and direct-worker paths both enforce this boundary.

Full **1UA2 was inspected, not prepared**. Its four TPO residues are now recognized as protein modifications, independently of its four ATP ligands. Four genuine **12-residue internal gaps** still require additional modeling; terminal missing sequence is also reported. The fragment test is not a substitute full-protein model. [1UA2 inspection](audit/modified-residue-1UA2-inspection.json)

SEP/TPO/PTR use fixed dianionic phosphate templates; changing requested pH does not establish their microscopic pKa or bound-state populations. Unsupported amino acids, terminal states, and covalent crosslinks remain explicit blockers. Prepared modifications currently use explicit TIP3P/OpenMM. Native numerical agreement does not validate rotamers, loops, metal coordination, thermodynamics, or long-time stability. Applying the distinction between algorithmic representations and populated molecular states to this workflow is this review's inference, informed by the Pat-authored [The Trouble With Tautomers](https://patwalters.github.io/The-Trouble-With-Tautomers/).

The blocker remains applicable to promoting scientific accuracy or convergence claims; passing software tests do not remove it.
