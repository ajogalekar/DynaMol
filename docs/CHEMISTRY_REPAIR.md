# Chemistry repair and viewer sizing

In Simulate, incomplete ligand cards list the missing atoms and offer **Build missing atoms from chemical reference** or **Remove this molecule from prepared structure**. **Repair missing ligand atoms** selects repair for all eligible retained ligands. These choices are explicit and are recorded in the preparation job; the original dataset remains unchanged.

Repair uses the complete wwPDB Chemical Component Dictionary graph and stereochemistry. It fixes every observed heavy-atom coordinate while modeling a small missing acyclic fragment. The current local bounds are eight missing heavy atoms per ligand, no more than half its heavy atoms, a connected observed core with at least three noncollinear anchors, and no missing ring atoms. Extra atoms, ambiguous identity, unsupported covalent attachments and larger/core omissions remain actionable errors. A bounded seeded conformer search, local relaxation, stereochemistry checks and a gross-overlap screen are applied before normal GAFF2/AM1-BCC parameterization. Generated atom names, positions, seed, candidates, method and uncertainties are saved. Modeled coordinates are starting hypotheses, not experimental observations or validated binding poses.

Named monatomic ion states, including Zn²⁺, are checked against the selected installed Amber TIP3P ion template and an actual OpenMM system. Element, deposited charge when available, template, particle charge and Lennard-Jones parameters must agree. Preparation checks the assembled system again. These nonbonded ion parameters require explicit water and do not model bonded coordination, charge transfer or changes of oxidation state. Unknown or ambiguous identities are not assigned whichever template happens to match.

The protein loop checkbox is a selectable preparation preference. When retained sequence records show no missing protein residues, the UI says that there is nothing to add. Numbering skips are distinguished from physical backbone breaks. Ligand atom repair is a separate choice; the protein loop option does not complete an incomplete ligand. Existing protein loop length, identity and terminal-region restrictions remain unchanged.

The handle below the molecular canvas resizes the view vertically in Explore and Simulate. Drag it, or focus it and use the arrow keys; Shift makes a larger step, Home/End select the size limits, and Enter or a double-click restores automatic sizing. Chrome remembers the chosen height locally. The minimum keeps the viewer toolbar visible, and the canvas resizes with its panel.

Validation includes a complete preparation of an isolated 6A93 monomer with its Zn ion and all ligands retained. Both incomplete 1PE instances were repaired (three and six heavy atoms); the complete instance stayed complete. All 98 original ligand and ion heavy atoms retained their exact saved coordinates. This check used two CPU threads with optional side-chain refinement disabled, and performed no dynamics integration. The detailed software checks, provenance and model limitations are in [the chemistry repair audit](audit/chemistry-repair/).
